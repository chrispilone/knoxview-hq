Knox View HQ privacy policy

Last updated November 5, 2026.

Knox View HQ (hq.knoxview.com) is an internal business tool used only by Knox View Media LLC and its team. It is not offered to the public.

QuickBooks data

When the owner connects QuickBooks Online, HQ reads financial reports (Profit & Loss, Balance Sheet) and company information to show a finance summary to the owner. HQ only reads data; it does not create, change or delete anything in QuickBooks. Sign-in tokens and the latest summary are stored in Knox View Media's private database and are visible only to the owner.

Sharing

We don't sell or share this data with anyone. It is used only to run Knox View Media's own business.

Disconnecting

The owner can disconnect QuickBooks at any time from the Finance tab in HQ. Disconnecting revokes HQ's access and deletes the stored tokens and summary.
